About FERMATA

Simple Route

What FERMATA does

FERMATA helps people discover public transport stations, understand connected routes, and find the most relevant nearby options for a journey in Addis Ababa.

What it does not do

FERMATA is not a general-purpose navigation product for every city or every trip type. It focuses on public transportation discovery and route understanding.

Data quality

When verified route data is unavailable, the product clearly shows that route information is limited and avoids presenting fake or invented transport routes as fact.
